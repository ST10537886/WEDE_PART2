Our beginning

LizDeLux Botanicals is a developing South African handmade soap and body-care business. It began in 2025 as Made by Liz and is evolving into a broader botanical brand led by founder and product maker Lizzy Maake.

Mission

To produce quality botanical body-care products that encourage simple, enjoyable and responsible self-care.

Vision

To become a trusted South African body-care brand recognised for quality, transparency, thoughtful presentation and customer-centred service.

Who we serve

The brand serves adults interested in locally made, small-batch soaps and botanical body-care products.
